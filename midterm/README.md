## Files

- `exam.pdf`: your exam as it was scanned.
- `exam.html`: every part with your points, a transcript of what you wrote, the scan of
  your answer and the grader's comment. Download it and open it in a browser (GitHub
  shows .html files as source).
- `scores.csv`: the same, one row per question part. `transcript` is your handwriting as
  read from the scan image; `ocr_read` is the first-pass text recognition (often garbled),
  or the bubbles/circles read as filled. `graded_from` says whether a written answer was
  graded from the recognized text (`ocr text`) or from the image of your handwriting (`image`).

If anything looks misread or misgraded, comment on your Gradebook issue with the question
number and what you wrote.
